How the result was tested

  1. Twenty non-refrigerated SKUs over 28 days.
  2. Both policies open with the same stock.
  3. Reactive ordering buys only after stock reaches zero.
  4. StockWise reviews every three days, in full packs, within cash and storage limits.

Sustainability and scale

  • Use existing phones. No new scanning equipment is required.
  • Protect privacy. Shop codes replace names; no customer personal data is stored.
  • Add more shops. Shop-level records and role checks support multi-site growth.
  • Keep people in control. Suggestions are provisional when demand data is weak.
